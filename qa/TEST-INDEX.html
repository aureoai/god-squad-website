GOD SQUAD — local test index Real .liquid files rendered against mock Shopify data. Not a Shopify store.

Start here — run it as a site you can click through

This is the better way to check the design. One command maps the theme's real Shopify routes onto the rendered pages, so the site behaves like a site: click a product card and you land on the product page, click the cart and you get the cart, and an unknown URL gives the theme's own 404.

cd "C:/Users/TEST/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-TEST-OneDrive-Documents-GodSquad-Website/de238d03-508d-43ce-a377-210f71ff0033/scratchpad" && python serve-site.py

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000. A small bar at the bottom shows which route you are on and which fixture it resolved to, with shortcuts to every state. It does not need the two servers below.

The routing is the only fake part. Every page is the theme's own Liquid, CSS and JavaScript. But Shopify decides which product a URL resolves to, and this maps each handle to whichever fixture best demonstrates that kind of product — so the price on the card you clicked may not match the page you land on. The fixtures exercise the theme; they are not a consistent catalogue.

What works here, and what cannot — read this or you will file false bugs

BehaviourWorks locally?Why
Layout, typography, colour, spacing, responsive yes Real CSS, real rendered Liquid. Resize the window freely.
Mobile menu, cart drawer, filter drawer, accordions yes Real JavaScript, no server needed to open and close them.
Variant picker, quantity stepper, product gallery yes Client-side. Swatches and sizes switch, the stepper clamps.
Keyboard navigation, focus rings, reduced motion yes Tab through anything. Esc closes drawers.
Add to cart / update / remove no Needs Shopify's /cart/*.js endpoints. There is no backend — the request fails and you will see the theme's error state, which is itself correct behaviour.
Real search and real filtering no Shopify does the querying. The pages below are fixed renders of each result state.
Checkout no Shopify-hosted. Nothing in the theme can simulate it.
Photography placeholders The pages below show six placeholder images — the prototype's own crops, copied in by the harness build. They are AI-generated with unconfirmed rights and are small (the hero is 713×401 shown at 1265px wide, so it looks soft). The theme ships zero images and no merchant photography exists. So judge composition and the shape of the image slot, not the picture quality — and expect the finished site to look materially different here.

4 — What to actually look for

Do thisBecause
Narrow the window to ~375px on the cart page and the collection page The drawer/row switch is at 768px and the filter UI becomes a drawer below it. A live typography defect in Phase 18 was visible only at phone width.
Tab through the homepage without touching the mouse Skip link first, then header, then content. Every control should show a visible ring.
Open the cart drawer, then press Tab repeatedly Focus must stay inside the drawer and Esc must close it and return focus to the button that opened it.
Open a filter group, then a product accordion All three disclosure chevrons should point down closed and up open. That was wrong in two of three places until Phase 18.
Click "Add to bag" It will fail — there is no backend. What you are checking is that it fails gracefully: a readable message, no raw error, no stuck spinner.
Judge the image slot, not the image The pictures are low-resolution prototype crops standing in for photography that does not exist yet. Their softness and their cropping are artefacts of the placeholder, not of the theme.
Scroll slowly the first time Images below the fold are lazy-loaded, so a band can look empty for a moment before its picture appears. That is the loading strategy working, not a missing image.

5 — Testing it properly the only way to see the real flow

Nothing above tests the purchase flow, because the purchase flow is Shopify's. To see the site actually working end to end it has to go on a real store — zipped and uploaded, then configured in order. §10 of GODSQUAD-THEME-REFERENCE-MANUAL.md is the nine-stage runbook for exactly that, including the five things that can only be verified against a live store.

The automated suites are the other half: 199 structural checks, 74 contrast measurements, and the interaction assertions for cart, product and drawer lifecycles. They cover what clicking cannot.
